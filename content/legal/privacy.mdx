## 1. Introduction

Welcome to DeepUrl. We are the infrastructure that powers seamless connections between websites and mobile apps.

When you use our deep linking technology, APIs, SDKs, or visit our website (collectively, the "Services"), you are trusting us with your data. We see ourselves as the guardians of that information, not just processors.

## 2. Data We Process

To ensure your links function correctly across different devices and operating systems, we need to process specific data points.

### A. Account & Administrative Data

- **Credentials:** Your work email, GitHub/SSO login tokens, and developer dashboard passwords.
- **Billing Profiles:** Invoicing addresses and payment tokens.
- **Support Queries:** Debugging logs or screenshots you upload when opening a ticket.

### B. System-Generated Telemetry

- **Digital Fingerprints:** User-Agent strings, OS versions, and browser engines.
- **Network Signals:** IP addresses and carrier information.
- **Interaction Metrics:** Timestamps of clicks, app-open success rates, and redirect latencies.
