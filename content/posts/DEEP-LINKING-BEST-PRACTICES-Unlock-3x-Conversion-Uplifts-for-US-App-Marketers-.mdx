Deep links are predefined URLs engineered to steer users to the specific in-app destination upon activation.

Beyond enhancing user experience, they enable developers to achieve granular attribution for marketing campaigns, tracking and measuring performance from installs to targeted actions.
More than user friendly, they also facilitate developers tracking the success of their marketing campaign.

These links extend functionality across channels — including web traffic, email campaigns, push app notification, display and search ads, social media posts, or other apps — directing users directly to the optimal screen for funnel optimization and sustained conversion uplifts.

## Why Deep Linking Delivers ROAS Rocket Fuel (Key Perks Backed by Data)

Deep linking boosts mobile engagement among users, seamless experience drives higher conversion rates (like 3x), uplifts ROI, strengthens retention and precise tracking of user behaviour and journey.


**Streamlines efforts towards success :**\
Once you implement deep linking in your marketing strategy, single click from user results in app visit and precisely saying, before the desired content. No guessed path, no irritated customers, no manual force to locate themselves to specific page.

**Superior Marketing Analytics :**
Beyond strengthening engagement, you can also track the source of this conversion, or purchase. Just one action gets enough capable of improving your ROAS ( return on ad spend) along with accurately attributing the touchpoint for the engagement.

**Improved Retention and Re-engagement:**
Notifications, Email marketing are crucial tools of digital marketing. Deep links in such tools can even bring inactive users back to your app or product, improving brand visibility and engagement.

### &#xD;&#xA;BEST PRACTICES FOR DEEP LINKING-

**CONFIGURE CORRECTLY -**
To make the user journey as seamless as possible, you need to ensure correct in-app configuration for precise routing. This encompasses non-display of any login pages or pop-ups which might change the mood of the user. Additionally, you must verify that the redirect fallbacks are set up guaranteeing that the user will always be navigated to the right content regardless of the device or link they hit.

**DEEP LINKS ON MULTIPLE CHANNELS -**
Deep links across various platforms are helpful in boosting user acquisition and engagement. From Ads to social media campaigns to QR codes, when deep links are embedded everywhere, conversion probabilities increase with guiding users effortlessly from one touchpoint straight to the in-app content.

**NO BREAKING LINKS :**
An app remains in a constant state of change - whether new feature launch or debugging. If the user hits the deep link directing to a specific page which no longer exists - you might land in serious trouble, losing a customer. As app updating rate of users vary, a portion of users will land on the desired page while rest on empty space. Thus, ensure your deep links are well tested to avoid broken bridge.

**BRANDED LINKS:**
Customize your URL with your specific brand name or website in order to differ it from standard links. It instills confidence in users as a credible link to click. You can use these branded links in social media posts, email marketing and other platforms to increase CTR( click through rate) and engagement.
