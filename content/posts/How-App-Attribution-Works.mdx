## HOW APP ATTRIBUTION WORKS :

**INTRO:**

In the fast paced tech world, data is the new oil and “attribution” is refinery. Getting a user to download your app is not the only battle but which Ad campaign actually made that conversion is also an indispensable info.
So, let’s take a closer look at exactly what it is, how it works and who are the key players.

### WHAT IS ATTRIBUTION?

Attribution is the process of mapping the user’s path to determine what all within the app influenced the user’s action and conversion - allowing marketers to assign credits to specific touchpoints - such as Ad clicks, QR codes, social media posts which led to in-app action.
![](</uploads/ChatGPT Image Mar 16, 2026, 03_45_13 PM.png>)

#### **&#xD;&#xA;APP ATTRIBUTION?**

Simply put, app attribution or mobile attribution is the process of mapping how users engage with your mobile app and ascertain which marketing touchpoint created the user’s action (like an app install or purchase).
Marketers use Mobile analytics tools and MMPs (Mobile measurement partners) like Deepurls for app attribution. These partners provide SDKs (Software Development Kits) to track metrics of your app such as installs, user engagement and other interactions.

### **HOW APP ATTRIBUTION WORKS?**

User’s Click/View: A user interacts with an advertisement (e.g., clicks a tiktok ad).

Tracking Link: The ad carries a tracking link that sends user data (like device ID, IP address) to MMP, like DeepURL.

App install: The user installs and launches the app.

SDK Match: The MMP’s **Software Development Kit (SDK)** within the app logs the install and matches it with the earlier click data.
Attribution: The MMP attributes the specific marketing campaign and reports it to the advertiser/marketer.

#### **OTHER TYPES OF ATTRIBUTIONS:**

Apart from app attribution or mobile attribution, other types of attributions based on your specific marketing goals also exists such as-

**Web attribution** **-** It maps and analyzes the data about how users engage with your website. Web attribution analytics tools facilitate tracking web traffic, user behaviour metrics, CTRs (Click through rates) as well as bounce rates.

**Cross channel attribution-** Marketers use multiple channels to promote and advertise to reach customers.

This attribution maps the entire journey of the user - across devices and platforms to read each and every touchpoint till the conversion.

Marketers can identify high performing networks, gain comprehensive understanding of different channels and improve overall marketing efficiency.

**Full funnel attribution -** It encompasses comprehensive tracking of the entire customer journey, from awareness to conversion and retention. Full funnel insights are important to understand the effectiveness of marketing endeavours at every stage of user journey, resulting in more calculated and strategic decision making.

#### **THE KEY PLAYERS -**

* **ADVERTISER / MARKETER :**
  That’s you (the app owner).
* **THE PLATFORM :**
  Google, Tiktok or any other platform where your Ad is live.
* **THE MMP :** Mobile Measurement Partner like Deepurls which identify, analyse the data and tell you which network contributed in winning the conversion.

### MMPs and SDKs

Most developers don't build their own tracking systems. Instead, they join hands with **Mobile Measurement Partners (MMPs).**
These partners provide a **Software Development Kit (SDK) —** a small piece of code that developers "plug in" to their app. This SDK acts as a lens, facilitating marketers with a clear view of their campaign performance across different platforms and devices.\\

## **MARKETING ATTRIBUTION CHALLENGES -**

Attribution models variety, fragmented customer journeys, signal loss due to privacy regulations (GDPR) General data protection regulation, (CCPA) California consumer privacy act, altogether make it difficult to accurately measure ROI and map full multi device, multi channel path of user engagement like purchase.
Even though you could track every touchpoint accurately, you'd still face a question: which interaction/click actually deserves attribution for the conversion?


**CONCLUSION:**

Attributions are not only about tracking people, it's about measuring signals. Attributions are crucial for marketers to gauge the effectiveness of their marketing strategy and allocate the resources at the right place efficiently. Marketers who stay ahead of the curve in this rapid technology advancement era gain competitive upper hand and drive maximum ROI and ROAS along with providing satisfactory user experience.

#### **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:**

* **What is the use of attribution in mobile apps?**

Attribution in a mobile app identifies which marketing campaign drive app installs and post install activities by tracking user's journey - enabling marketers to attribute the touchpoint that drove the action.

* **What is a touchpoint in customer journey?**

Any point a consumer interacts with your brand, whether through your website, or app is known as touchpoint. For instance, a customer sees an eco-friendly product ad on tiktok. That is customer touchpoint No. 1 with that business.
